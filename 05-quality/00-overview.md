# Quality

This section covers the quality standards that separate a good skillbook from a great
one — self-review practices, common pitfalls, and the art of right-sizing pages.

## When to Read This Section

- You've finished writing content and want to polish before publishing
- You're reviewing someone else's skillbook
- Your pages feel too long or too short and you need tuning guidance
- You keep hitting the same validation errors

## Pages in This Section

- `01-self-review-checklist.md` — A pre-publish quality gate to run through before shipping
- `02-common-mistakes.md` — Frequent authoring mistakes and how to fix them
- `03-page-length-tuning.md` — When to split, when to merge, and finding concept boundaries

Read `01-self-review-checklist.md` when you think you're done. Read the others when
you're stuck on a specific quality problem.
